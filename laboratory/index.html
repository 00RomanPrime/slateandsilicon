Try This First

5 Minutes of Silence

Set a timer for 5 minutes. No phone. No screen. No audio. Sit with it. Notice what your mind reaches for, and when. You're not trying to relax. You're reading the baseline.

Duration: 5 min // Observation only
Try This Next

10 Minutes, No Phone

Leave your phone in another room. Not silenced. Removed. Wait 10 minutes. Notice when the impulse to check it arrives. Notice what triggered it. That's the data.

Duration: 10 min // Observation only

If either one produced something unexpected, that's the starting point.

Home-Screen Friction

Phase 03 Boundary Building High Friction Days 15–20

You're moving from noticing automatic entry to constructing something sturdier around it. This phase tests evening closure, notification authority, and physical distance from the phone — boundaries built on purpose, in daylight, before you need them.

EXP-07 In Development

Evening Shutdown

Three nights. Screen-based visual input ends at a shutdown point you choose and hold for all three nights. Not a sleep requirement — a clear end so the day stops being indefinitely extended by screens.

EXP-08 In Development

Notification Kill

One setup day, then a background condition that holds for the rest of the protocol. Turn off everything that isn't a genuine, immediate need — calls, texts, safety and work essentials stay on.

Behavioral Ongoing Phase 03
EXP-09 In Development

Phone Distance

Three continuity blocks. The phone leaves the room — not silenced, physically removed — for one meaningful task at a time. Retrieving it for ordinary checking ends the block; that's a result, not a failure.

Phase 04 Runway High Friction Days 21–23

Boundary construction is behind you. This phase prepares for the final feed closure by moving social media out of continuous background availability and into a place you choose — consolidated, budgeted, then narrowed to one last window before it closes.

EXP-10 In Development

Digital Off-Ramp

Three staged days. Consolidated access, then a time budget, then one final planned window before the fast begins. Social media stays available throughout — access just stops being a background default.

Analog Morning

Three mornings, running alongside the Off-Ramp. One physical, non-screen action before the digital environment enters — protecting the day's first movement before the feed defines it.

Phase 05 Expanse Full Stack Days 24–30

Social Media Fast

Seven consecutive days. No feed-first consumption — no feeds, reels, shorts, or profile wandering. Direct communication stays open. Communicate directly. Don't enter the feed.
